CURRENT ISSUE

The capabilities folder currently mixes three different concepts:

1. Product workflows / executive activities
2. Business capabilities
3. Reusable AI engines

We need to separate these.

DEFINITIONS

Product = what the executive is trying to accomplish.
Examples:
- Executive Briefing
- Project & Opportunity System
- Meeting Intelligence
- Document Intelligence
- Automation Engine

Capabilities = reusable business-level AI capabilities.
Examples:
- Research
- Writing
- Decision Support
- Sales Intelligence
- Relationship Intelligence
- Presentation Generation
- Marketing

Engines = lower-level reusable AI services used by capabilities and product workflows.
Examples:
- Reasoning Engine
- Retrieval Engine
- Search Engine
- Memory Engine
- Learning Engine
- Document Analysis Engine
- Meeting Analysis Engine
- Relationship Engine
- Automation Engine
