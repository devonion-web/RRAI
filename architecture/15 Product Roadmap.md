# 1. Purpose

This document defines the strategic evolution of the RRAI platform.

Unlike the preceding architecture documents, which describe what RRAI is, the Product Roadmap defines how RRAI will mature over time.

It should be reviewed regularly and updated as priorities evolve.

The roadmap guides investment, engineering priorities and product direction while ensuring alignment with the Product Vision.

---

# 2. Vision

RRAI will evolve into the world's leading Executive AI Operating System.

The platform will progressively expand from an intelligent executive assistant into a configurable enterprise intelligence platform capable of supporting multiple organisations, executives and AI agents.

Every release should move the platform closer to this vision.

---

# 3. Guiding Principles

Every roadmap decision should:

Support the Product Vision.

Strengthen the Executive Operating System.

Improve executive productivity.

Increase organisational intelligence.

Enhance Memory.

Expand Learning.

Improve capability reuse.

Preserve architectural integrity.

Features should never compromise the architecture.

# Release Philosophy

Each release should:

Improve existing capabilities before adding new ones.

Strengthen Memory.

Improve Learning.

Increase stability.

Preserve simplicity.

Maintain architectural integrity.

Small, high-quality releases are preferred over large disruptive releases.

---

# Product Governance

The roadmap should be reviewed:

Quarterly for strategic priorities.

Monthly for delivery planning.

Continuously for architecture alignment.

Major roadmap changes should be reviewed against:

Product Vision.

Executive Operating System.

Design Principles.

Architecture Handbook.

No roadmap item should compromise the long-term architecture.
